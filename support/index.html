Here to help

BrainWod support

Email info@tontech.co with “BrainWod” in the subject. Include your device model, app version and a description of what happened. Never send passwords, payment details or private store credentials.

How do the puzzles work?

Open Settings → How to play for instructions. Each session has four rounds of Memory, Numbers and Focus. Continue moves to the next round after you have read the answer.

Where is my progress?

Progress is stored on your device. BrainWod has no account or cloud progress sync. Reinstalling the app or deleting its data can remove your history. Device backups may behave differently by platform.

Reminders and text size

Choose a daily reminder in Settings and allow notifications in your device settings. You can also increase your device text size and enable Reduce Motion.

Purchases

Where Premium is available, use Restore purchases with the same store account. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple or Google account. Deleting BrainWod does not cancel subscriptions. Contact your store for payment and refund requests.

Privacy or deletion requests

Settings → Delete data on this device clears your local progress and preferences. For questions about support messages or information held by a service, email info@tontech.co. You do not need a BrainWod account to contact us.
